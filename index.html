About

I'm Kowshik, a freelance video editor in Bangalore who also writes scripts and designs graphics. I can take a project from first idea to finished video, or step in for just the part you need.

My scripts are built around what keeps people watching: a hook in the first seconds, pacing that holds attention, and a clear call to action at the end. I then edit to match, so the words and the cuts work together.

Because one person does all three, there are no handoffs between a writer, a designer and an editor. That saves you time and money, and you only ever talk to me.
